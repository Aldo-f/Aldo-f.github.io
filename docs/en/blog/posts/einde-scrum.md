It's Wednesday, the 5th day after the scrum week.

In my opinion, it moved too fast. Not that the project wasn't ready - with the minimal functionality - but it's just hard to see a project as "done".

## How did it go?
First we figured out who would take on what, and we mostly worked per page/functionality.

### Who did what?
* Griet:
  * Pages: registration, own profile, edit profile, view user, ...
  * Functionality: input validation, photo upload, zodiac sign display, ...
* Mesut:
  * Pages: messages, index, navigation
  * Functionality: everything related to messages (posting, reading, sorting, deleting, starting)
* Wouter:
  * Page: search, navigation
  * Functionality: everything related to search (number per page, search according to ..., ...) and the pagination at the bottom.
* Aldo:
  * Pages: navigation, registration, login (in navbar), own profile, favorites, lovecoins (actions and adjustments), ...
  * Functionality: login, localStorage, favorites, lovecoins (actions and adjustments), ...
