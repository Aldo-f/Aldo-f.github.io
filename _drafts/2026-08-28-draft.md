This week saw the integration of the urbanfix submodule and the exposure of the letspeppol UI via Traefik, highlighting the continuous expansion of the home lab's service mesh and the careful orchestration of new components into the existing Traefik network.

The documentation site received a new feature - the FreeLLM API chat widget - and underwent a reorganization, reflecting the ongoing effort to make the personal knowledge base more interactive and accessible.
